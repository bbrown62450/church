# Voices of the Church PR V1: the Catena Aurea on the Readings Step

**Goal:** Ship Voices of the Church V1 (owner's answers of 2026-10-01, planning answers and source decision of 2026-10-05; planning answer 8: "V1, the rights check, the text import and the Readings panel"). After it merges, step 1 of the Service Builder (`/builder/readings`) shows a **Voices of the Church** panel under the Gospel reading's row: collapsed, its button reads "Voices of the Church" and "19 quotations on these verses"; open, it shows each section of Thomas Aquinas's Catena Aurea that shares a verse with the passage, with its verses and a link to its printed pages in the scan, then each father's comment exactly as the 1841-45 Oxford printing has it (the father's name, the margin reference such as "Chrys. Hom. lxx.", the text with its italics), and the credit to the edition. A section nobody has checked against the page images yet says "Not yet transcribed for this passage." The passage is a bulletin reading from a Gospel; when neither bulletin reading is one, the Sunday's lectionary Gospel, labeled "From the Gospel for this Sunday: Matthew 22:15-22" (planning answer 1). The text is four data files the app ships with (`backend/data/catena/`): every section of the four Gospels (Matthew 277, Mark 104, Luke 245, John 190) with its verses, volume and pages, and the text of the sections checked while planning, Matthew 22:15-22 (the owner's test Sunday, October 18, 2026) first. No database change, no AI, no new package or variable; production Streamlit (branch `streamlit-frozen`) is untouched. V2 ("Bring this into the liturgy" and the "after {Name}" credit) is designed in the spec and not built here.

**Architecture:** `backend/catena.py` (new, pure) reads a Gospel's file once (`lru_cache`), checks it against the format (`CatenaDataError`), and answers `sections_for(reference)` with the app's own reference parser (`scripture_refs.parse_refs`, the hymn matcher's) and `credit(gospel, sections)`. `backend/data/catena/{matthew,mark,luke,john}.json` hold one section a line (and a checked section's comments one a line), text only for checked sections; `backend/data/catena/README.md` is the checking checklist. `backend/scripts/catena_import.py` (new, standard library, never run by the app) turns archive.org's ABBYY OCR of the eight volumes into drafts (`fetch`, `draft`, `show`) and rewrites a data file keeping its checked sections (`index --checked FILE`). The API: `usecases/voices.py` and `api/routes/voices.py`, `GET /voices?reference=` (user-scoped like `GET /translations`; `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`; a 422 naming `reference` for a passage that is not in a Gospel); `VoicesOut`, `VoiceSectionOut`, `VoiceCommentOut` in `api/schemas.py`; OpenAPI regenerated. Frontend: `lib/voices.ts` (`voicesPassage`: which passage, from the draft's effective bulletin readings and the lectionary answer step 1 already holds), `lib/queries/voices.ts` (`useVoices`, key `["ref", "voices", reference]`, an hour), `components/builder/readings/voices-panel.tsx` (the panel, on the kit's `Collapsible`), placed by `readings-list.tsx` under the Gospel's row (`ReadingRow` gains `children`) or after the list.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 (`.venv`), FastAPI 0.141, Pydantic 2, pytest; Next 16, React 19, TypeScript 5, Base UI, TanStack Query 5, Vitest 3 with Testing Library; openapi-typescript for `schema.d.ts`.
