# Printed bulletin: owner idea (pre-spec)

Status: idea only, not a spec. Sequenced after slice 5a (owner, 2026-10-02):
5a-1, 5a-2, 5a-3, then the printed bulletin, then Voices of the Church, then 6a.

The owner wants the app to produce the real Sunday bulletin, not only the
Word working files. The sample is First Presbyterian Church's bulletin for
September 27, 2026 (a mailing PDF the owner shared in the session; not
committed).

## What the sample has that the app does not know yet

- Layout: a folded booklet, legal paper landscape, two pages per side. Cover
  with church name, an image, the scripture reference and date, and the
  church's address, phone, email, website and Facebook name. Last page is
  announcements.
- People: a header with the worship leader, liturgist and organist, and the
  leader's name right-aligned on each element.
- Music: prelude and postlude titles and composers.
- Sung response with its lyrics (Gloria Patri).
- Readings printed in full text (the app already fetches the text).
- Standing marks ("*HYMN", "*Congregation stands if able").
- Announcements: ushers and counters, deacon of the week, coffee hour, the
  week's activities, prayers and concerns, collection items.
- Benediction printed in quotation marks with "- Richard Halverson".

## Questions for the planning round

PDF or Word; which parts change weekly and which are church settings; how
the cover image is supplied; whether the mailing version differs from the
Sunday handout.
